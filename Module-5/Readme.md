
## CAS Applied Data Science - Module 5 - Consolidations
2025-11-21 Peer Review (Moderator Alberto)

Prerequisites: Your Module 3 notebook(s).

In this session you will randomly be assigned to a course colleague. The colleague will discuss with you her notebook(s) from the Module 3 project. Then you change roles. For this you have more than two hours, so you can really study each others work. The moderator can support you.

During the peer exchange and afterwards at home, you write a consulting report (PDF) for your peer colleague. The peer consulting report (PDF) is the assessment for Module 5. The deadline for uploading this report to the Reports folder here on Ilias is December 31. At the same time you also email the report to your colleague.

The report should be in English and 3-4 pages long with font size 11 and 1.5 spacing. It should contain sufficent metadata (your name, contact, etc, also of your colleague) and references you have used or suggest for your colleague.

First you describe the Module 3 task of your colleague in about half a page, then you point out good solutions (about one page) and aspects and then you suggest improvements (about one page).
